Kikkoman Vegetarian Ramen

_Proven, Japanese

Cook Time: total time 25 mins Servings: 2 portion(s) Source: kikkoman.eu

Ingredients

200 g tofu

300 ml sunflower oil

¼ carrot

160 g bok-choy

100 g leek

4 piece green beans

200 g ramen noodles

For the ramen Soup:

60 ml Kikkoman Concentrated Ramen Noodle Soup Base - Shoyu (Soy Sauce) Flavour

540 ml water

For the seasoning mix for Shiitake mushrooms

2 piece shiitake mushrooms

25 ml Kikkoman Concentrated Ramen Noodle Soup Base - Shoyu (Soy Sauce) Flavour

150 ml water

Directions

For fried tofu, drain the tofu with kitchen paper, put in microwave (700w) for 2 minutes, then fry in the oil for 10 minutes.

Bring the seasoning mix for shiitake to the boil and cook for 7 min.

Chop the carrots, leek and bok-choy. Boil each of these vegetables and the green beans separately until softened.

Prepare the noodles according to the packet instructions.

Bring approx. 540 ml water to the boil in a pan, mix with 60ml Kikkoman Ramen Soup Base.

Divide the soup into 2 bowls. Drain the noodles well and add to the soup.

Place the cooked shiitake, boiled vegetables and garnish with tofu.
