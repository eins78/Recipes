Vegan Pastrami-Style Tofu Sandwich

USA

Servings: 4 sandwiches Source: Instagram @jen.eats.plants

Ingredients

For the pastrami tofu:

400g medium-firm tofu, sliced into medium-thin slices

2 tbsp black pepper

1 tbsp ground coriander

1 tsp mustard powder

1 tbsp brown sugar

1 tbsp paprika

2 tsp garlic powder

2 tsp onion powder


For the coleslaw:

150g white cabbage, finely shredded

1 small carrot, grated

2 tbsp vegan mayo

1 tsp apple cider vinegar

0.5 tsp dijon mustard

salt + black pepper, to taste


For the russian dressing:

3 tbsp vegan mayo

1 tbsp ketchup

1 tsp sriracha

1 tsp pickle juice

0.5 tsp dijon mustard

0.5 tsp smoked paprika

1 tbsp finely chopped dill pickles

black pepper, to taste


To serve:

8 slices sourdough

vegan cheese

sweet pickle slices

vegan butter or oil, for frying

Directions

Mix the pastrami spice rub together in a bowl or Tupperware. Add the tofu slices one at a time, making sure each piece is thoroughly coated in the spice mix.

Place the tofu on a lined baking tray and bake at 170°C for 20 minutes, or air fry at 170°C for 15 minutes, until crispy on the outside but still soft and juicy inside. The tofu will firm up a little more as it cools.

For the coleslaw, mix the shredded cabbage and carrot with the vegan mayo, apple cider vinegar + dijon mustard. Season with salt + black pepper.

For the russian dressing, mix everything together until smooth and creamy.

Assemble your sandwich with vegan cheese, pastrami tofu, coleslaw, russian dressing + plenty of sweet pickle slices. Add the top slice of sourdough, then toast the sandwich in a pan with a little vegan butter or oil until golden and crisp.

Notes

From Jen's "from the sandwich shop" series: pastrami-style tofu with creamy coleslaw, russian dressing, vegan cheese + sweet pickles on sourdough.
